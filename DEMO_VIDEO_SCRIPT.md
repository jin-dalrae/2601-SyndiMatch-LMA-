# SyndiMatch Demo Video Script
## Duration: ~3 minutes

---

## [0:00-0:15] Opening Hook

**[Screen: Dashboard overview with active syndications]**

**Narrator:** "Loan syndication is a $3 trillion market, but it's still done manually—taking weeks, with limited transparency. Meet SyndiMatch: the first AI-powered platform that automates the entire loan syndication workflow in hours."

**[Screen: Close-up on dashboard metrics]**

**Narrator:** "Let me show you how it works."

---

## [0:15-0:45] Feature 1: Multi-Agent AI System

**[Screen: Switch to Platform Admin view, show agent orchestration]**

**Narrator:** "SyndiMatch uses five specialized AI agents that work together autonomously. The Originator Agent broadcasts a loan opportunity—say, a $500 million corporate loan for a tech company."

**[Screen: Click on a syndication card, show detail view]**

**Narrator:** "The system automatically recommends pricing based on credit rating and market conditions. Then, Participant Agents—representing institutional investors like Apollo Global, CalPERS, and pension funds—evaluate the opportunity against their risk profiles."

**[Screen: Show bidding activity feed with real-time updates]**

**Narrator:** "Each agent makes autonomous decisions: Should I bid? How much? At what spread? The AI considers portfolio fit, yield targets, and available capital."

---

## [0:45-1:30] Feature 2: Dutch Auction & Real-Time Bidding

**[Screen: Show auction progress chart]**

**Narrator:** "The Negotiation Agent runs a Dutch auction—starting at the initial spread and automatically lowering it each round until we find the market-clearing price."

**[Screen: Show subscription rate increasing in real-time]**

**Narrator:** "Watch the subscription rate climb as bids come in. We're at 45%... 67%... 89%... and we've hit 100% in just three rounds. The system automatically closes early when the target is met."

**[Screen: Show allocation results]**

**Narrator:** "For oversubscribed deals, the system allocates pro-rata. Here, Apollo gets $75 million, CalPERS gets $50 million, and so on—all calculated automatically."

---

## [1:30-2:15] Feature 3: Settlement & Blockchain Payments

**[Screen: Switch to Payments view]**

**Narrator:** "Once the auction closes, the Settlement Agent manages documentation, compliance checks, and signature collection—all tracked in real-time."

**[Screen: Show payment pipeline]**

**Narrator:** "Then comes the Payment Agent. It processes commitment fees, arrangement fees, and principal payments via Coinbase x402 on Base L2—using USDC for instant, gasless transactions."

**[Screen: Show transaction log with blockchain hashes]**

**Narrator:** "Every payment is recorded on-chain with full transparency. Here you can see the transaction hashes, confirmation status, and amounts. The system automatically handles escrow and releases funds to the borrower once all payments are confirmed."

---

## [2:15-2:45] Feature 4: Analytics & Business Value

**[Screen: Switch to Analytics view]**

**Narrator:** "The platform provides comprehensive analytics. Here's participant performance—win rates, average spreads, portfolio allocation. Market spread heatmaps show pricing trends across industries and credit ratings."

**[Screen: Show volume chart]**

**Narrator:** "And volume trends help originators understand market demand."

**[Screen: Return to overview, show multiple active syndications]**

**Narrator:** "The platform can handle multiple syndications simultaneously. Each one runs independently through the same automated workflow."

---

## [2:45-3:00] Closing & Value Proposition

**[Screen: Dashboard with success metrics]**

**Narrator:** "SyndiMatch transforms loan syndication from a weeks-long manual process into an hours-long automated workflow. Originators get faster closes and better pricing. Participants get access to quality deals with transparent, AI-powered matching."

**[Screen: Platform logo with tagline]**

**Narrator:** "The future of loan syndication is here. SyndiMatch—where AI meets finance."

**[Screen: End card with website/contact info]**
