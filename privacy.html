01Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Cadinal LLC (“Company,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, shares, and safeguards information in connection with the SyndiMatch platform (the “Platform”), including the website, the application, and the APIs. It applies to information of institutional users and the natural-person representatives of those institutions (employees, contractors, and beneficial owners we are required to identify).

It does not apply to information about borrowers, issuers, or other parties that you submit as part of a simulated deal record. You are responsible for ensuring that any such data is lawful for you to submit and that an appropriate basis exists under your own privacy program.

02Information We Collect

2.1 Account & identity

  • Institution name, legal entity type, and primary address;
  • Name, business email address, role/title, and business phone number of each Authorized User;
  • Authentication credentials, API keys, and access tokens issued for the Platform.

2.2 Usage & technical

  • Pages and views accessed, features used, session duration, button clicks, and other product-analytics events;
  • HTTP request metadata: IP address, user-agent, timestamp, referrer, and response code;
  • Server and application logs generated by the Platform’s Node and Python services, including error stack traces.

2.3 Submitted deal data

  • Syndication, bid, allocation, and payment records that you create or import through the Platform;
  • Free-text fields you submit for AI-agent evaluation (deal descriptions, borrower descriptions, internal memos).

2.4 Communications

  • Email correspondence, support requests, and feedback you send us;
  • Records of meetings, demos, or onboarding sessions, when conducted with your consent.
Compliance Agent

Four buckets: who you are (account info), what you do (clicks and request logs), what you upload (deal records and free-text fields), and what you tell us directly (emails, support tickets).

03Sources of Information

We collect information directly from you and your Authorized Users; automatically through your use of the Platform; and, where applicable, from sub-processors and analytics providers that operate on our behalf.

04How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Platform, including authentication, account administration, and customer support;
  • Operate AI agent workflows on data you submit (see Section 5);
  • Generate aggregated or de-identified analytics that do not identify your Institution or any individual;
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our Terms;
  • Comply with applicable law, lawful requests by public authorities, and our legal obligations;
  • Communicate with you about product updates, scheduled maintenance, beta program announcements, and material changes to these documents.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

05AI Processing

The Platform uses generative artificial-intelligence models supplied by third parties (currently Anthropic and Google) to power agent workflows, draft documents, and summarize activity. When you submit free-text fields or deal data for evaluation, that content may be transmitted to the relevant model provider for processing.

We configure our integrations to avoid model providers training on your data where the provider offers such a control. However, model providers retain logs for limited periods for safety, abuse prevention, and compliance purposes under their own terms. You should review the providers’ published policies before submitting sensitive data:

  • Anthropic API: Commercial Terms and Privacy Policy.
  • Google AI: Google Privacy Policy and AI-specific terms.

Do not submit material non-public information about issuers or counterparties through the Platform’s free-text fields during the beta.

Compliance Agent

When you type into a free-text field, it goes to Anthropic or Google for inference. We turn off training on customer data where we can. Do not paste MNPI into our text inputs during the beta.

06Sharing & Sub-Processors

We share information only as described below.

  • Sub-processors. We use third-party services to host and operate the Platform. Each operates under contractual confidentiality and security obligations.
    • MongoDB Atlas — database hosting (currently U.S. regions).
    • Google Cloud Platform — compute and storage for the Node API and Python agent service.
    • Anthropic — large-language-model API for agent reasoning.
    • Google AI (Gemini) — language-model API for summarization and reporting.
    • Coinbase Developer Platform — future real x402 payment processing (not active during the closed beta).
    • Firebase Hosting — static frontend distribution.
  • Professional advisors. Lawyers, accountants, and auditors engaged in connection with our business.
  • Successors. A party that acquires or succeeds to our business in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.
  • Legal compliance. Government authorities, regulators, and courts when we are required to do so by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of Cadinal LLC or any third party.
  • With your direction. Any other party at your written direction or with your explicit consent.

We do not sell or share personal information for advertising purposes.

07Cookies & Local Storage

The Platform uses browser localStorage to persist a small number of session settings (for example, a demo-mode toggle and the most recently selected role). We do not use third-party advertising cookies. Standard server logs may include cookies set by hosting providers for load-balancing and security purposes.

08Data Retention

We retain information for as long as your Institution’s account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter to satisfy legal, audit, and dispute-resolution obligations. Default retention windows during the beta are:

  • Account & identity: until account deletion, then up to 24 months for accounting and legal compliance.
  • Usage & technical logs: up to 90 days, except where retained longer for security investigation.
  • Submitted deal data: until you delete it through the Platform or request deletion, subject to operational backups (typically purged within 30 days).
  • Communications: up to 24 months from the last interaction.
Compliance Agent

Active accounts kept indefinitely. After deletion: technical logs 90 days, deal data ~30 days in backups, account & comms 24 months for audit and legal.

09Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. These include transport encryption (HTTPS), authentication and access controls, audit logging, dependency monitoring, principle-of-least-privilege for production access, and routine review of sub-processor security posture.

No system is fully secure. If you become aware of a security incident affecting the Platform, please contact us promptly at the address in Section 15.

10International Transfers

Our infrastructure is operated primarily in the United States. If you access the Platform from a jurisdiction with different data-protection laws, you understand that your information will be processed in the United States and other countries where our sub-processors operate.

11Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you and your Authorized Users may have rights to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Request deletion of personal information, subject to legal-retention exceptions;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Request a copy of personal information in a portable format;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in Section 15. We will verify the requester’s identity before responding and may require the request to come from an Authorized User of the Institution.

12California-Specific Disclosures (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act) provides additional rights. The categories of personal information we have collected in the past twelve (12) months map to the CCPA categories as follows:

  • Identifiers (Cal. Civ. Code §1798.140(v)(1)(A)): name, email, business address, IP address.
  • Internet or other electronic-network activity information (subsection (G)): pages visited, features used, request logs.
  • Professional or employment-related information (subsection (I)): job title, employer.
  • Inferences (subsection (K)): we do not draw inferences for the purpose of building consumer profiles.

Sources, purposes, and recipients of each category are described in Sections 2, 3, 4, and 6 above. We do not sell personal information within the meaning of the CCPA, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

California residents may exercise the rights described in Section 11. You may also designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.
