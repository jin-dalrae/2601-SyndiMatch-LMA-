## Slide 4: How It Works

### Five Specialized AI Agents

1. **Originator Agent** → Broadcasts opportunities with AI-recommended pricing
2. **Participant Agents** → Evaluate and bid autonomously based on risk profiles
3. **Negotiation Agent** → Runs Dutch auctions for optimal price discovery
4. **Settlement Agent** → Manages documentation and compliance
5. **Payment Agent** → Processes payments via blockchain (x402 on Base L2)

**Result**: End-to-end automation from broadcast to fund distribution
