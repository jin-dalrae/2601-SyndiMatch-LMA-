What we commit to log

  • Every agent decision — the prompt, the model version, the inputs, and the structured output.
  • Why it decided that way — the chain of reasoning produced by the model, recorded verbatim.
  • On whose behalf — the institution, the agent role, and the human Authorized User who initiated the workflow.
  • Reversibility — whether the action was committed to MongoDB, broadcast to participants, or kept as a draft.
